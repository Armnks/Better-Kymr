## Collection-wide signature (applies to every prompt)

Precise art direction; tactile real-world materials; warm ivory light or graphite shadow;
controlled vermilion only where noted; strong negative space; restrained fine film grain;
contemporary camera language; believable physics.

**Never include:** recognizable logos or brands, packaging text, any embedded text or
lettering, watermarks, hands with wrong finger counts, duplicated objects, warped geometry,
decorative smoke, impossible reflections, generic AI fantasy rendering.

**Explicitly banned compositions** (these are the current library's formula and must not recur):
perfume bottle on black stone · headphones floating on black · empty beige bedroom · syrup
poured over a surface · gadget on a pedestal · silver can with water splash · watch on a dark
rock · stationary black supercar · cosmetic jar on beige blocks · glasses floating in smoke ·
skincare jar beside cream · isolated white shoes on concrete.

### H1 — `hero-01-concept.webp`
**Stage:** The idea.
**Prompt:** A creative treatment wall photographed flat-on in raking early-morning window
light: printed photographic frames pinned in a loose grid, a marked-up contact sheet with

grain. No legible text anywhere — annotation marks read as gesture only. 3:2.
**Alt:** A pinned treatment wall of printed frames and marked-up contact sheets in morning light.

### H2 — `hero-02-production.webp`
**Stage:** The making.
**Prompt:** On-set production still: a hand pouring water in a slow controlled stream, lit by a
single hard key from camera left, with the edge of a white bounce card and the corner of a

Graphite background, warm ivory highlight on the pour, one small vermilion mark on a piece of
set tape. Honest anatomy, five fingers, natural skin. Documentary camera language, fine grain. 3:2.
**Alt:** An on-set pour lit by a single hard key, with bounce card and flag visible in frame.

### H3 — `hero-03-output.webp`
**Stage:** The system.

different formats — one tall vertical, one square, one wide — arranged slightly overlapping on

across one print's corner. Even soft top light, honest paper texture and edge shadows, fine

**Alt:** Prints of one campaign frame in several formats laid out together, sharing one grade.

### 01 · Fragrance — **The Hour Before**
- **Premise:** The concept explores fragrance as a private ritual of preparation rather than a cue for seduction — the ten minutes before leaving, not the encounter afterwards.
- **Audience:** Self-purchasers 25–40 who buy scent for themselves, not as a gift.
- **Objective:** Designed to move a scent out of the gifting calendar into a repeatable personal routine.
- **Hook:** The opening hook would hold on the empty space where the ritual happens, before any product appears.
- **Tone:** Private, unhurried, slightly tense with anticipation.
- **Motif:** Human behaviour and time — the product implied, never hero'd.
- **Lighting:** Single cool window source, deep falloff, no fill.
- **Colour:** Cold bathroom neutrals against one warm skin note.
- **Composition:** The edge of a mirror with condensation, a hand's motion blur leaving frame, the bottle present only as a sharp shadow cast on tile. Product deliberately out of focus at frame edge.
- **Extensions:** Could extend into a set of time-of-day cuts sharing one grade and one sound bed.
- **Deliverables:** 6s and 15s paid social · 9:16 and 1:1 masters · static key art · hook variants
- **Prompt:** `fragrance-01-the-hour-before.webp` — A bathroom mirror edge with fine condensation, cool morning window light raking across wet tile. A hand blurred in motion exits the right of frame. On the tile, the crisp cast shadow of a small faceted bottle — the bottle itself sits soft and out of focus at the extreme frame edge. Cold neutral greys, one warm skin tone, deep unfilled shadows, fine grain, strong negative space. Honest anatomy, five fingers. No text. 3:2.
- **Alt:** A condensation-covered mirror edge with a blurred hand leaving frame and a bottle's shadow on tile.
